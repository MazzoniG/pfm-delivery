Rules for this screen

  1. The sidebar gains a two-item navigation: Transactions and Reports. It lists only screens that exist. Accounts, balances and net worth stay exactly as in the balances mock-up.
  2. The URL is the state. from and to are dates, rounded to whole months by the range control; month is the selected bar. A missing month means the latest month in range. The contract's projectId parameter is not exposed here.
  3. One bar per month in the range, from the API's month series. A month with no spending is still a bar at zero, never a gap. The current month is marked as partial. Clicking a bar selects that month and writes month. Bar heights may use a JS number for geometry only; every figure a user reads is the API's string through the formatter.
  4. Expense categories only, sorted by amount. A row expands to its transactions, fetched when opened. The share bar is presentation, scaled against the largest row; it carries no figure.
  5. Amounts here are seen from the category's side of the entry: spending is positive and a reversal is negative. The register shows the same purchase as negative because it looks from the bank account. Both are correct; do not flip either one.
  6. A category total that contains a correction carries an Includes a correction badge. Inside, correction rows use the register's own badge component and link to the original the same way.
  7. The month total comes from the API, not from summing the rows.
  8. Lines excluded from reporting are absent from every figure here while still counting in balances. The footnote says so, since the two will not always match.

Rules for these states

  1. A negative category total is correct, not a bug: a correction posted this month reversed spending reported in an earlier one. It keeps its minus sign, never passes through an absolute value, and carries one line saying why.
  2. A month with no spending still has a bar, drawn as a baseline at zero. Selecting it shows an empty state rather than an empty table, and the copy covers both reasons a month can be empty.
  3. Loading keeps the chart's and the table's geometry so nothing jumps. Shimmer stops under reduced motion.
  4. Errors show the detail from the problem+json body as written, the same rule as the register and balances.
