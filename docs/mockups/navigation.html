Rules for this pattern

  1. Global navigation moves out of the accounts panel into its own rail. The panel keeps net worth, the as-of control and the accounts, and nothing else about it changes.
  2. The rail expands over the content and never pushes it. Nothing in the accounts panel or the register reflows when it opens.
  3. It opens after a short delay and closes after a slightly longer one, so crossing it on the way to an account doesn't flash it open.
  4. Keyboard: it expands whenever focus is inside it. Items are reachable with Tab in order, and the current screen carries aria-current="page".
  5. Touch has no hover, so the rail stays collapsed there. Every icon link carries an accessible name, and the current screen is highlighted in both states.
  6. Pure CSS: :hover, :focus-within and transition delays. No component state, nothing in Zustand, nothing in the URL. There is nothing to persist, so there is no pin control.
  7. Only screens that exist appear. Motion is removed under reduced-motion preferences.

Rules for these states

  1. Open, the rail shows the wordmark and each item's label beside its icon, with a soft shadow separating it from the content underneath. The current screen keeps its highlight.
  2. Opened by keyboard, the focused item shows the focus ring and the rail stays open for as long as focus is inside it. Leaving with Tab or Shift-Tab closes it.
